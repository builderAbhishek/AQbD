Numerical Optimization Report¶

The “Report” is the most detailed of the optional views of optimization outcomes. It presents its results in several sections:

  • The constraints on the design space

  • Factors and responses and the goal for each.

  • A list of solutions (the optimum solutions found).

  • A list of the random starting points where the searches began. (You may be interested in this list if you wish to verify that the space was thoroughly searched.)

Design-Expert eliminates multiple starts that lead to the same optimum, so fewer results than the number of starting points may be reported. The list of solutions will be sorted with the highest desirability first. Only solutions that meet the criteria are reported.

Note

If the program finds no solutions, perhaps you set the criteria too tightly. Loosen them up and try again. Tradeoffs are a necessary part of optimization. Another option is to start with the most important response and set all the other goals to None. Do you get a solution? If so, add the goal for the next most important response, and so on.
